Beyond the requirements

The specification asked for inventory, purchases, sales, a dashboard and one AI feature. We went further in six areas.

Global UX

Beyond a single-language app

  • 6 languages with full Arabic RTL mirroring
  • Dark / Light themes with zero flicker
  • Culture-safe number formatting
  • Themed dialogs replace browser popups
Reports & finance

Business insight, not just lists

  • Inventory valuation report
  • Sales report: revenue, order value, top products
  • One-click CSV export
  • Printable, numbered sales invoices
Productivity

Faster daily work

  • Instant global search for products and categories
  • Low-stock alerts in the top bar
  • Live POS checks: no duplicates, no over-selling
  • Supplier-driven product picker
Data safety

History can never be corrupted

  • Delete guards explain why a record is protected
  • Voiding a sale or purchase reverses stock
  • Price snapshots keep past revenue accurate
  • Exact decimal money and unique SKUs
AI depth

More than a chatbot

  • Answers grounded only in live database facts
  • Sales velocity and dead-stock detection
  • Summary reports generated in chat
  • Rule-based fallback if the LLM is offline
  • Saved chat history, sanitized replies
Engineering

Enterprise-grade foundations

  • Interface-based services with dependency injection
  • Anti-forgery protection on every write
  • Strict view models block over-posting
  • Reusable paging and result patterns
